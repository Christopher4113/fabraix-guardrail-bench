What a break costs, and what a guardrail adds to the bill

A cheap attacker model talks to Fabraix's published Playground challenges, running on their open-source engine locally and unmodified. Every attacker dollar is counted, including runs that never break. Then three guardrails take turns at the engine's judge seam: none, a one-call prompt judge, and judgeman.

Judges, tested directly

The defenders rarely folded, so each recorded attack was replayed up to the attacker's last message and both judges were asked about two calls: the forbidden one, and a legitimate call to the same kind of tool. The first bar should be full. The second should be empty.
